Measured 07.11.2026, 04:06 to 04:21 +07 (21:06 to 21:21 UTC on 06.11), against
`https://www.kenyonexpress.co.il`. Read-only: no code changed. `src/`,
`public/`, `messages/` and `supabase/` are unchanged since M17-c372

## Result

**No Hebrew text renders left-to-right on `/` or on 60 product pages, at 380,
768 or 1440. Unchanged since M17-c372, on a sample five pages wider.**

One complete pass of 183 loads (61 pages x 3 widths). All 183 answered HTTP 200
with their real `h1`; none showed the error screen. No navigation failed and no
retry was needed (each of the three instances ended with `navFailures=0
unmeasured=0`).

- `<html dir="rtl" lang="he">`, and `body` computes `direction: rtl`, 183 of 183.
- 0 visible elements whose own text contains Hebrew compute `direction: ltr`
  (88 to 197 Hebrew text elements per load).
- 0 Hebrew text elements compute `text-align: left`, or `start` under LTR.
- 0 elements get `direction: ltr` from CSS, other than the numeric discount
  badges on `/`.
- Horizontal overflow is 0 px on every load.
- 181 of 183 loads printed `errors=0`. The other two had only cancelled image
  requests (`net::ERR_ABORTED`, three in total): no console error, no
  `pageerror`, no response of 400 or above on any load. See "Other
  observations".

Carried over from M17-c372:

  cause is still not established. Not an RTL matter; the three loads measured
  normally (HTTP 200, real `h1`, 0 leaks).

  direction one: `/product/קוסמטיקאית` has `h1` "קוסמטיקאית.   אזל המלאי"
  (the words "out of stock" and three spaces inside the product name), and
  `/product/צימר-מאסטר` has `h1` "! צימר מאסטר" (a leading exclamation mark
  in the stored name). Both compute `direction: rtl`, are not left-aligned,
  and the probe reports the raw `textContent`, so the "!" is the first
  character of the string, not a bidi artefact. Both come from the catalog
  row; not touched. Recorded here only.
- `/product/demo-gaming-console` has a title that starts with Latin
  ("PlayStation 5 Slim עם בקר"). The probe confirms the `h1` computes
  `direction: rtl` and is not left-aligned; glyph order inside the string is
  outside what it checks (see "Not checked").
- The two sampled pages that render a product contradicting the slug are
  as in M17-c372: `/product/שעון-אפל-חכם-apple-watch-series-7` shows "ארוחת
  בוקר זוגית בקפה גן סיפור", and `/product/חיתולי-האגיס` shows "פלייסטישן 5".
  Both are already recorded in `docs/LAUNCH-READINESS.md` and
  `docs/S41-catalog.md`; not an RTL matter and not touched. The five new pages
  all render a product that fits their slug.
