## Verdict
- Storefront allowed (`Allow: /`, no blanket `Disallow: /`); no staging noindex leak.
- All private/credential routes disallowed; each exists under src/app (gift, scan, cart, checkout, reset-password, forgot-password, coupon, redeem, voucher, debug).
- Not blocked: product/category pages.

## Observation (not a robots defect)
Prod NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL is the apex `https://kenyonexpress.co.il`, but
`curl -sI https://kenyonexpress.co.il/robots.txt` -> 308 to https://www.kenyonexpress.co.il/robots.txt.
So Sitemap/Host, sitemap <loc> and the home canonical (`https://kenyonexpress.co.il`) all point at the
redirecting host. Crawlers follow the 308, so robots works; fix is to set NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL to
`https://www.kenyonexpress.co.il` in Vercel env (or flip the redirect direction) at next deploy.
