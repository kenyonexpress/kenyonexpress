## Warnings, recorded and not fixed

Unchanged from M15-c362. All 12 warnings in pass 2 (and all 10 in pass 1) are
Chromium's "preloaded using link preload but not used within a few seconds"
notice, at 1440 only: 9 for three CSS chunks and 3 for `/images/logo.webp`.
They are warnings, not errors, and the logo preload lives in
`src/app/layout.tsx`, which is frozen. The count moves between runs with load
timing (16 and 4 in M15-c362).

On `/product/אייפון-13` at 380, the only load traced in detail, one image had
not finished loading at the end of 9 of 10 traced loads: the footer logo,
`loading="lazy"`, measuring 0x0. It is not requested in those loads, so it is
neither broken nor an error.
