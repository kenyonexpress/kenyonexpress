## Product bugs fixed (these reach customers)
- **Email sign-in and sign-up never navigated** (a83bbde8b). `/api/auth/login` and
  `/api/auth/signup` delegate to actions that end in `redirect()`. A route handler answers
  that with `HTTP/1.1 303 See Other` + `location: /account` (curl-verified). `fetch` follows it, so
  the form parsed the destination's HTML as JSON, threw, showed "שגיאת רשת" and stayed
  on /login. The forms now follow `res.redirected` → `res.url`. New `LoginForm.test.tsx`
  fails without the fix and passes with it.
- **Raw driver errors shown at checkout** (94ffe66d5): `שמירת פריטי הזמנה נכשלה: TypeError: fetch failed`
  and three siblings that put `error.message` in the customer text. They are now logged
  (`checkout.order_items_insert_failed` and siblings), and the shopper sees "לא ניתן לשמור את ההזמנה כרגע, נסו שוב".
- **Shekel sign left of the digits in product names** (efc401580). Catalogue text
  `שובר 200 ₪ למעדנייה` measured ₪ at x=84 against the digit at x=116. `repairPriceOrder` now also
  isolates `<digits> ₪` and is applied to card, deal-card, cart, mini-cart and drawer titles. Unit tests added.
- **HotCouponCard countdown contrast** (efc401580): `text-orange-600` on white failed axe
  color-contrast x4 on `/`. It is now orange-700, and the copied-confirmation text is green-700.

## Harness and tooling fixes
- `e2e/otp-seed.ts` + `scripts/seed-test-data.mjs` (6f9f6ac8f): `listUsers` page 1 is newest
  first. Anonymous guest sign-ins (216 users, 198 anon) pushed the fixtures off it, so

  `SyntaxError: Identifier 'parseLedger' has already been declared` (an auto-merge left an import and a local copy).
  The comment-blanking scanner was unreachable dead code. Now it passes:
  `hardcoded-gate: no new hardcoded values beyond docs/hardcoded-audit.md`. Plus import order
  and format in 5 files, and `Reflect.deleteProperty` in TurnstileWidget.test (`delete` on
  process.env was a biome error; `= undefined` would store the string "undefined").

  category thumbs measure laid-out boxes only (5d43e254b); home-rtl resolves and reads in one
  polled evaluate (274c6363b); a11y checkout retries the pay click (378b21c7f).
