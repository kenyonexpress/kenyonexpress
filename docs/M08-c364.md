## The link

Measured from this machine with no throttling, during the session:

```
$ curl https://<project>.supabase.co/rest/v1/products?select=id&limit=1  (3 fetches, 05:23Z)
  connect 2.05 s, TLS done 11.2 s, total 25.6 s
  connect 3.68 s, TLS done  5.5 s, total  7.6 s
  connect 0.29 s, TLS done  0.65 s, total 1.9 s
$ curl --compressed https://www.kenyonexpress.co.il/  (3 fetches, 05:42Z)   connect 0.26-2.62 s, ttfb 1.02-4.42 s
$ curl --compressed https://www.cloudflare.com/       (3 fetches)           connect 0.24-0.52 s, ttfb 0.75-2.09 s
```

TCP connect alone took 2 to 3.7 s to two different hosts in some fetches, and an unrelated site took up to 2.1 s to its first byte. Connect time involves no application work, so that part is the path (the VPN), not Supabase and not Vercel. It does not rule out a server-side share: six of the 13 live runs reported a document server response above 400 ms where c363 had two of 12.

## Runs the link damaged

- **Local build, attempts 1 and 2: exit 1.** 210 and 217 requests to Supabase passed the 10-second client timeout. The reads that fail open (reviews, stock) logged and carried on; the read behind `product page (supplier google reviews)` (`src/lib/product-detail.ts:296` via `src/lib/supabase/optional-columns.ts:76`) throws, so one timed-out product page ends the build. `/contact` failed the same way in attempt 2. Attempt 3 passed with 192 timeouts, so passing depended on which reads timed out. M06-c364 built the same `src/` an hour earlier with 6 timeouts and exit 0.
- **`live-home-devtools-2`: invalid.** Lighthouse warned "The page loaded too slowly to finish within the time limit" and returned no TBT and no performance score (`NO_TTI_NETWORK_IDLE_PERIOD`). It is excluded from the median and replaced by run 4.
- **`live-home-devtools-1`: SEO 92.** The `robots-txt` audit failed with "Fetch of robots.txt failed: Timed out fetching resource". `curl https://www.kenyonexpress.co.il/robots.txt` answered `200 text/plain` three times out of three (1.1-1.7 s), and the audit passed in the other 12 live runs.
- **`llms-txt` (Agentic Browsing category, not one of the four above).** Failed in three live `/` runs with the same fetch timeout, which put that category at 67 in those runs and 100 in the other 24. When the fetch completes the audit is not applicable: `/llms.txt` answers 404, live and local.
