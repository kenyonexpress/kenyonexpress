```

/newsletter/confirm  200  meta robots "noindex, nofollow"
/partner/redeem      200  meta robots "noindex, nofollow"
/debug/sentry        200  meta robots "noindex"   (see below)
/debug/sentry/render 200
/api/debug/sentry    404
/dev                 404
/zzz-not-a-page      404
```

`/debug/sentry` and `/debug/sentry/render` answer 200, and the gate is off.
The body carries `NEXT_HTTP_ERROR_FALLBACK;404` and none of the debug panel
text, and the route handler under `/api/` answers a real 404. The 200 is the
streamed `notFound()`: both pages call it inside a Suspense boundary (required
by `cacheComponents`), after the status line has gone out. Next adds the
`noindex` meta itself, and `/debug/` is disallowed, so nothing is crawlable or
indexable. It is a soft 404 on two unlinked URLs, not a robots problem, and
nothing was changed.
