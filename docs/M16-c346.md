## Observation (not a gate failure)
Raw HTML of a product page contains the ld+json scripts twice (4 total, 2 Product + 2 BreadcrumbList): once inside the pending Suspense boundary `<!--$?--><template id="B:7">` in `<main>`, once in the streamed `<div hidden id="S:7">` that `$RC("B:7","S:7")` swaps in. After the swap the DOM holds one copy, so a rendering crawler sees one; a non-JS parser sees duplicates. `/faq` and `/c` emit 2 (no duplication). Cause is the streaming boundary above `<main>` in the (store) layout/PDP, not the JSON-LD builder. Not fixed here: out of scope for a verify item and the layout area is shared.
