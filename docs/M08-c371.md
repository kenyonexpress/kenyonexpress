The round trip is 230 ms at best and jumps past a second, to an unrelated host as well as to `www`. Lighthouse's devtools throttling adds its own delay on top of the real link, so a real round trip of 0.3-1.3 s lands on every request of a throttled run. These pings were taken after the runs, there is no ping from c370 to compare with, and nothing was measured from a second network, so this stays a candidate explanation. Supabase was not timed directly.
