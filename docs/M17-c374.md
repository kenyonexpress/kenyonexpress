## Result

**No Hebrew text renders left-to-right on `/` or on 65 product pages, at 380,
768 or 1440. Unchanged since M17-c373, on a sample five pages wider.**

One complete pass of 198 loads (66 pages x 3 widths). All 198 answered HTTP 200
with their real `h1`; none showed the error screen. No navigation failed and no
retry was needed (each of the three instances ended with `navFailures=0
unmeasured=0`).

- `<html dir="rtl" lang="he">`, and `body` computes `direction: rtl`, 198 of 198.
- 0 visible elements whose own text contains Hebrew compute `direction: ltr`
  (88 to 197 Hebrew text elements per load).
- 0 Hebrew text elements compute `text-align: left`, or `start` under LTR.
- 0 elements get `direction: ltr` from CSS, other than the numeric discount
  badges on `/`.
- Horizontal overflow is 0 px on every load.
- 197 of 198 loads printed `errors=0`. The other one had a single cancelled
  image request (`net::ERR_ABORTED`): no console error, no `pageerror`, no
  response of 400 or above on any load. See "Other observations".

Carried over from M17-c373:

  rendered the product at all three widths, and no load had an `h1` of
