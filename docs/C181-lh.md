## What does not, and why

1. **SEO 69 on /cart and /checkout.** The only failing audit is `is-crawlable`, which fails on the `Disallow: /cart` and `Disallow: /checkout` lines in `src/app/robots.ts`. Lighthouse fails `is-crawlable` on a robots.txt disallow and on a noindex meta tag alike, so 90 needs both pages to be indexable. C152 already decided against that, and so do F25 and docs/LIGHTHOUSE-AUDIT.md. I did not change it. `canonical`, the other failing audit, is fixed (61 -> 69).
2. **Perf on signed-in routes**: cart 1440 70, checkout 380 89, checkout 1440 87, account 1440 84 (medians). These are per-user renders, and their TTFB is Supabase round trips from this machine (iPhone hotspot, Thailand) to the staging branch. Staging REST time-to-first-byte measured 0.83 / 2.74 / 1.25 s during the run. The proof: `/cart` is a fully static page with zero page reads, and it answers in 6 ms anonymous but 1.5-2.3 s signed in. The difference is the one `auth.getUser()` in `src/proxy.ts`. Swapping it for local JWT verification would change ban and revocation semantics to win a lab score, so I did not.

Earlier C-items measured those two routes as `/cart` and `/login`.

All four categories. Per the project recipe, the quoted score is `--throttling-method=devtools`. `simulate` (Lantern) is recorded too, but on localhost it charges the whole page to LCP: home's observed LCP was 524 ms while simulate reported 5.0 s.
