## Reading

- **Performance, local.** Devtools throttling (4x CPU, 150 ms RTT, 1.6 Mbps applied for real) gives 99 on `/` and 98 on the product page: LCP 1.6 s and 2.2-2.3 s, TBT at most 60 ms, CLS at most 0.003. M08-c361 measured 99/99. The product page's one point is LCP moving from 1.75-1.8 s to 2.2-2.3 s. Two runs per side are not enough to call that a regression, and no code under the page changed in between. Simulate again spreads 9 to 11 points on one build, the known Lantern-against-localhost pattern, so devtools is the figure to quote.
- **Performance, live.** Devtools median 95 on `/` and 84 on the product page, with a wide spread (79-95 and 70-90) over three runs each. The swing is in the network leg: under throttling the LCP breakdown shows time to first byte of 1.1-2.3 s across the 12 live runs, against an unthrottled server response time of 291-314 ms in every one of them. TBT stays at or under 109 ms in devtools runs and CLS at most 0.003, so the main thread and layout are as steady live as they are locally. Three runs through a VPN do not support a tighter statement than "roughly 85-95, network-bound".
- **Accessibility 100 and SEO 100** on both pages, local and live, in all 26 runs.
- **Best Practices 100 on `/`**, local and live. The local 96 of M08-c361 is gone with the `VERCEL=` override, which confirms its cause (the `/_vercel/*` tags rendered off Vercel). Live, those scripts load.

## Best Practices 96 on the live product page

One audit fails, `errors-in-console`, with the same four entries in all six live product runs:

```
network | Failed to load resource: the server responded with a status of 400 ()
  /_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fcdn%2Fwp%2F48%2F488b903a...webp&w=288&q=75   (demo-vacation-package)
  /_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fcdn%2Fwp%2F6f%2F6f25d9ae...webp&w=288&q=75   (demo-tzimmer)
  /_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fcdn%2Fwp%2F09%2F09a48c49...webp&w=288&q=75   (demo-hotel)
  /_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fcdn%2Fwp%2Fac%2Fac93cb7f...webp&w=288&q=75   (demo-flight)
```

These are the images of the four related-product cards at the bottom of the page. The source files do not exist:

```
$ curl https://www.kenyonexpress.co.il/images/cdn/wp/48/488b903a...webp        -> 404 text/html
$ curl ".../_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fcdn%2Fwp%2F48%2F488b903a...&w=288&q=75" -> 400 INVALID_IMAGE_OPTIMIZE_REQUEST
  (same 400 at w=256, 384 and 640, and at w=288 with an image Accept header)
$ ls public/images/cdn                                                         -> No such file or directory
$ git ls-files public/images/cdn/wp | wc -l                                    -> 0
```

A shopper on this product page sees four related cards with broken images. This is the defect already written down in `docs/G4-launch-seed.md` ("production answers 404 for them") and counted in `docs/S41-catalog.md` (39 demo first images point at `/images/cdn/wp/...`). What is new here is that it is now measured on the live site, on a real product page, and that it costs the Best Practices score.

**The local 100 on the product page is not a clean result.** The local build requested the same four URLs and got `200 image/avif`. Those answers came from the optimizer's on-disk cache (`.next/cache/images`, `X-Nextjs-Cache: HIT`), not from a source file: the same URL at `q=60`, which is not cached, answers 404 locally, and so does the source path. A fresh checkout would show the same four errors locally.

Not fixed in this item. The data is the `demo-*` catalogue rows, and whether that catalogue stays is the open decision S41 left with Ofir (`scripts/remove-demo-catalog.ts`). Removing the rows, or giving them images that exist, clears the four errors.
